Digital Curb Cut Examples

Broad everyday technologies that benefit everyone

1

Closed Captions

Origin: Designed for the D/deaf community.
Universal Benefit: Used in noisy environments (gyms, bars), for language learning, and by people who prefer reading while watching.

2

Voice Control & Dictation

Origin: Created for people with limited mobility or dexterity.
Universal Benefit: Powers Siri/Alexa, hands-free driving, and quick text-to-speech for busy multi-taskers.

3

Dark Mode / High Contrast

Origin: Designed for light sensitivity and visual impairments.
Universal Benefit: Reduces eye strain for everyone, saves battery on OLED screens, and is a major aesthetic preference.

4

Autocorrect & Autocomplete

Origin: Assisted typing for people with motor impairments.
Universal Benefit: Essential for fast mobile typing and reducing typos for everyone.

5

Audiobooks & Text-to-Speech

Origin: "Talking Books" for the blind.
Universal Benefit: Massive mainstream market for commuting, exercising, and multi-tasking.
